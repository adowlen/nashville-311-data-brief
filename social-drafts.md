## Held copy - lead with the killer number

21.1% of Nashville's closed property-violation 311 requests in 2025 took more than 30 days to close (4,343 of 20,564).

248,066 requests opened with hubNashville in 2025 - about 680 a day. A third (78,753) were resolved on the first call, all of them closed the same day. Take those out and the rest of the system closes 44.0% the same day, median 1 day. Property violations are the slow lane: median 6 days, 90th percentile 64 days.

Full brief, data, and audit: https://github.com/adowlen/nashville-311-data-brief

## Variant (two clocks angle)

Nashville 311 runs on two clocks. First-call resolutions: 100% closed the same day. Everything else: median 1 day, but 10,131 requests took more than 30 days - and every one of them sits in the topic-type population, not the first-call one.
