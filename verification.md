## Pre-flight audit (HARD GATE - Section 10) - PASSED, findings resolved as follows

Full output: data/audit_output.txt (script: code/audit_section10.py). Findings and dispositions:

1. **Categorical exhaustiveness (Section 10.1.4):** full Counters recorded before any filter was chosen - Status 15 values, Request_Type 17 values, Subrequest_Type 134 values, Request_Origin 1 value (blank). Near-duplicate type spellings found and reported as recorded in the brief, never silently merged: "Public Works WO" (4,069) vs "Public_Works_WO" (2,686); "Parks" (150) vs "Parks and Environment" (1,760); "Planning and Zoning" (63) vs "Planning & Zoning" (13).
2. **Zero/null sentinel handling (10.1.1):** no numeric rate is computed in this brief, so no zero-to-bucket mapping exists. Missingness is handled as its own population everywhere: records without a closed date (7,669) are excluded from every gap figure and counted in the brief; records without a district (79,426) are excluded from district shares and counted in the brief.
3. **Sentinel-date / polarity check (10.1.2):** matched-vs-excluded sample is the date population itself: all 248,066 opened timestamps fall inside calendar 2025 on Chicago local dates (asserted in the audit); 0 records carry a closed date before their opened date; only 2 opened timestamps sit exactly on a UTC midnight, and the opened and closed UTC hour censuses span the full day (recorded in the audit output), so no midnight-placeholder pattern is present. Gap polarity: gap = closed local date minus opened local date, negative count 0.
4. **Numerator/denominator unit agreement (10.1.3):** every share in the brief is requests / requests inside a printed population (all 2025 opened, closed, district-recorded, or a named type's closed set). No cross-unit rate appears. District shares use the district-recorded population (168,640) as denominator, printed in the brief.
5. **Nullable-denominator trap (10.1.5):** type shares are computed over the full 248,066 opened population (the type field is populated on every record); the not-district-recorded share (32.0 percent) is printed alongside every district figure family, and the determined + not-determined counts sum to the whole (168,640 + 79,426 = 248,066, asserted).
6. **Status vs closed-date cross-tab (internal control):** 240,390 records carry Status "Closed"; 240,397 carry a closed date; the 7-record difference is exactly the dated records carrying the statuses Accept (2), Canceled (2), and Regret (3) as recorded. The brief prints both counts and this reconciliation.
7. **Missingness is systematic, stated prominently:** district missingness concentrates in the first-call type (68,610 of 78,753, 87.1 percent) and in Public Safety (17.7 percent), against 0.4 to 1.4 percent for the large field types. The brief therefore restricts district claims to the district-recorded population and says why, in the district section itself.
8. **Request_Origin:** blank on all 248,066 records (Counter has one value, the empty string). No channel claim is made anywhere in the brief; the gap is a Who-can-act item.
9. **IDs:** OBJECTID unique on all 248,066 rows; Request # unique on all rows and populated on all rows.
10. **Full-series control (built-in control group, Section 10.2.2 analogue):** server-side opened counts by Chicago calendar year (2017: 30,196; 2018: 82,991; 2019: 155,426; 2020: 239,784; 2021: 317,027; 2022: 260,797; 2023: 272,959; 2024: 277,019; 2025: 248,066; 2026 partial: 236,672) sum exactly to the live full-series count 2,120,937 - the year partition and the 2025 window are consistent with the service's own totals. No spatial or relational join is used in this brief, so the cross-street snapping checks do not apply; the district field is carried by the source records themselves, not geocoded by this package.

## Methods checklist

- [x] Dataset, agency, snapshot date, and record count stated in the brief (Metropolitan Government of Nashville and Davidson County via hubNashville; November 8, 2026; 248,066 in the 2025 population; 2,120,937 full series)
- [x] Unit of analysis defined (one record = one service request; a closed date records system closure, not field work performed)
- [x] Coverage and exact date range stated (opened January 1 to December 31, 2025, America/Chicago; snapshot November 8, 2026, so late-2025 requests have had less time to close - printed with the unclosed counts as a right-censoring note)
- [x] Base rates and exposure: no per-capita or per-household district rate is published because the layer carries no district exposure denominator; counts, shares of the district-recorded population, and closure timing only, with the absence stated in the brief
- [x] Direction of causality considered: closure timing by type is reported descriptively; the "What this might mean" section carries the strongest alternative (long recorded gaps may measure case workflow, not resident waiting) alongside the primary reading
- [x] Category and definition changes checked: type and status values are reported as recorded, spelling variants are named in the brief, and the year-to-year series movement is explicitly not interpreted because the layer publishes no definition history and its early years coincide with the system's ramp-up
- [x] Joins: none. Missingness for the source-carried district field is reported with its systematic pattern (finding 7 above)
- [x] Small numbers: percentages are printed with their underlying counts throughout; the smallest population carrying a headline-style percentage is Electric & Water General (950 closed), flagged in the brief with its population; types under 30 closed records carry no timing percentage in the brief
- [x] Derived figures labeled: the per-day figure (about 680 a day) and the one-in-five rendering of 21.1 percent are labeled derived in the brief
- [x] Official definitions: no city plan or standard is compared against, so no external definition is cited; the layer's own field descriptions are the definitional source and their limits are stated
